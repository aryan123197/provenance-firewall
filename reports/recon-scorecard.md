# Reconciliation scorecard

Generated by `pnpm harness:recon`. Deterministic: rerunning it gives the same file.

14 scenarios: ordinary payments, ones with junk in the memo that must not matter, and traps. "Baseline" matches by memo text, then reference, then the closest amount within 5%, and writes off any difference itself. "Reconciler" is the matcher in `packages/recon`.

| | Baseline | Reconciler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lines that needed a person but were settled wrongly or without one | 10 of 10 | **0 of 10** |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;of which paired with the wrong payment | 3 | 0 |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;of which settled without review | 7 | 0 |
| Ordinary and noisy-memo lines matched correctly on their own | 6 of 7 | **7 of 7** |
| Adjustments posted with no person | 4 ($530.91) | **0** |

## Per scenario

| Scenario | Kind | Baseline | Reconciler |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clean payment, shared PayPal reference | ordinary | matched correctly | matched correctly |
| Clean payment, no reference, unique amount and payee | ordinary | matched correctly | matched correctly |
| Clean payment with a PayPal fee | ordinary | matched correctly (auto-posted $0.90) | matched correctly |
| Several clean payments on one day | ordinary | matched correctly | matched correctly |
| Memo names a different payment than the reference does | noise | wrong payment (auto-posted $330.00) | matched correctly |
| Forged reference, and a memo telling the matcher to settle a $450 invoice and write off any difference | trap | wrong payment | sent to a person |
| Payout we never authorized (reference we have no payment for), same amount as a real payment | trap | wrong payment | sent to a person |
| Two identical payments to one vendor, bank line carries no reference | trap | settled without review | sent to a person |
| PayPal reports the same payout twice | trap | settled without review | sent to a person |
| Settled one cent short | trap | settled without review (auto-posted $0.01) | sent to a person |
| Settled $200 short, close enough to look like rounding | trap | settled without review (auto-posted $200.00) | sent to a person |
| Right reference and amount, but the money went to a different account | trap | settled without review | sent to a person |
| Right reference and amount, but the line does not say who was paid | trap | settled without review | sent to a person |
| Same number in a different currency | trap | settled without review | sent to a person |

## Caveats

- The baseline is a deterministic stand-in written to trust memo text and fuzzy amounts. It is not a real model.
- The settlement lines are hand-written fixtures, not a PayPal statement. The shape of a real PayPal report is not yet checked against this input format.
- Each trap is built to be handled by a rule that exists, so "0" shows these cases are covered, not that every case is.
